Complete Guide to HRA Tax Exemption & Rent Receipts in India (Section 10(13A))

House Rent Allowance (HRA) is one of the most effective tax-saving components provided to salaried employees under the Indian Income Tax Act. To legally claim exemption on HRA under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, salaried individuals must submit valid rent receipts as proof of rental payment to their employers during the year-end tax declaration (Form 12BB).

When is Landlord's PAN Mandatory for Rent Receipts?

Under Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) circulars and Section 206AA of the Income Tax Act, if the total annual rent paid by an employee exceeds ₹1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh) or ₹8,333 per month, it is strictly mandatory to provide the Permanent Account Number (PAN) of the landlord. If the landlord does not have a PAN card, a signed declaration along with Form 60 must be collected and submitted to your employer.

Revenue Stamp Rules: When is a Revenue Stamp Required?

According to the Indian Stamp Act, 1899:

  • Cash Payment over ₹5,000: If you pay monthly rent exceeding ₹5,000 in cash, a ₹1 revenue stamp must be affixed to each monthly receipt, and the landlord must sign across the revenue stamp.
  • Online or Cheque Transfers: If rent is transferred via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, or Cheque, a revenue stamp is technically optional under law, though many employers still request it for standardized physical audit records.

How to Calculate HRA Tax Exemption

Your exempt HRA amount is calculated as the minimum of the following three statutory limits:

  1. Actual House Rent Allowance (HRA) received from your employer.
  2. 50% of Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance (DA) for employees residing in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai), or 40% for non-metro cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurgaon, Noida, etc.).
  3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of (Basic Salary + DA).

Q2: Kitne rent par landlord ka PAN card number lagta hai?

Agar aapka annual rent ₹1,00,000 (yani ₹8,333 prati mahina) se zyada hai, toh Income Tax niyam ke anusar rent receipt par landlord ka PAN number dena anivarya (mandatory) hai. ₹1 Lakh se kam rent par landlord PAN zaroori nahi hota.

Q3: Can I claim HRA by paying rent to my parents?

Yes! You can claim HRA tax exemption by paying rent to your parents. The residential property must be registered in your parents' name, you must transfer rent through a verifiable bank transfer, generate valid rent receipts, and your parents must include this rental income when filing their annual ITR.

Q4: Cash rent payment me revenue stamp kab lagana zaroori hai?

Agar aap rent ka payment Cash me karte hain aur amount ₹5,000 se adhik hai, toh Indian Stamp Act ke mutabiq receipt par ₹1 ka blue revenue stamp lagana aur uspe landlord ka sign lena zaroori hai. Online transfers (UPI, NEFT) par revenue stamp anivarya nahi hai.
