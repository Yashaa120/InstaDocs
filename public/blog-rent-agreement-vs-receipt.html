1. The Fundamental Legal Difference

Parameter Rent Agreement Rent Receipt
Legal Nature Contract establishing tenancy relationship Voucher acknowledging financial consideration
Signatories Both Landlord and Tenant (+ witnesses) Landlord only
Stamp Duty State non-judicial stamp paper (₹100 to 0.5%) ₹1 Revenue Stamp (if cash > ₹5,000)
Frequency Once per tenure (typically 11 months) Every month or quarterly

2. What Does Employer HR Demand for Form 12BB?

  • Rent < ₹3,000 / month: Form 12BB declaration usually accepted without receipts.
  • Rent ₹3,000 to ₹8,333 / month: Monthly rent receipts required; agreement optional.
  • Rent > ₹8,333 / month (> ₹1 Lakh/year): Both Landlord PAN and Rent Receipts mandatory. Most employers also mandate the Rent Agreement.
  • Rent > ₹50,000 / month: Tenant must deduct 5% TDS under Section 194-IB and submit Form 16C certificate.
