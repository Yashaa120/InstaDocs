When Do You Need a Rent Affidavit?

A Rent Affidavit or Rental Self-Declaration is commonly required when:

  • Claiming HRA with Parents: When living in a parental house where formal commercial rental lease agreements are rarely drafted.
  • Missing Registered Rent Agreement: When an employer or bank requires formal verification of residential address and monthly tenancy.
  • Address Proof Verification: For passport applications, voter ID issuance, or bank account opening in a rented city.

Key Clauses Included in Our Rental Affidavit Draft

  • Deponent Identification (Tenant and Landlord full legal names, fathers' names, and age).
  • Clear description of the rented residential premises (Flat/House No., Society, Landmark, PIN).
  • Agreed monthly tenancy consideration and payment mode.
  • Solemn affirmation under oath that statements are true and accurate.
