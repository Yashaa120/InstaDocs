What is a Tenancy Self-Declaration Affidavit?

A Rent Agreement Affidavit is a sworn legal statement executed by a tenant or landlord affirming the existence of a tenancy, monthly rental terms, and actual physical occupancy of a residential premises.

Under the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 and the Notaries Act, 1952, an affidavit sworn before an Oath Commissioner or Notary Public serves as formal primary evidence of factual occupancy when a traditional registered lease deed is unavailable or pending execution.

When Do You Need a Rent Affidavit Instead of a Lease Deed?

While registered rental agreements are standard for formal leases, affidavits are indispensable in several common real-world scenarios:

Scenario Why Traditional Lease Fails Affidavit Solution
Living with Parents (Parent HRA) Parents and adult children rarely execute formal commercial leases with security deposits. A self-declaration affidavit affirming bona fide tenancy and monthly rent transfers satisfies HRMS audits.
Informal Shared Accommodation (Bachelors/PG) Only one master tenant is named on the primary landlord lease; flatmates lack individual leases. A tenancy self-declaration affirms co-tenancy and specifies the individual monthly rent share.
Passport & Police Verification Regional Passport Offices (RPO) require registered deeds if less than 1 year; unexpired 11-month deeds may face scrutiny. A notarized affidavit on stamp paper confirms present residential address under oath.
Bank Account & KYC Updates Private and PSU banks mandate address verification matching Aadhaar or rental documentation. A landlord declaration affidavit affirms tenant residence for utility and billing setups.

Essential Clauses in an Indian Tenancy Affidavit

To ensure statutory acceptance by government departments, banks, and corporate HR portals, our generator incorporates all compulsory legal declarations:

  • Deponent Identification: Full legal name, age, father's/spouse's name, permanent address, and Aadhaar/PAN identification.
  • Rented Premises Description: Complete postal address including flat/room number, apartment complex, municipal ward, and PIN code.
  • Tenancy Term & Rent: Exact commencement date of occupation and agreed monthly rent amount in Indian Rupees (₹).
  • Mode of Payment Affirmation: Declaration stating whether rent is transferred via banking channels (UPI/NEFT/Cheque) or cash.
  • Statutory Verification Clause: Formal sworn statement affirming that the contents are true and correct to the best of the deponent's knowledge, and nothing material has been concealed.
