1. The 3-Condition Statutory Formula

Under Section 80GG of the Income Tax Act, 1961, the deduction allowed is the LEAST of the following:

  1. ₹5,000 per month (Capped at ₹60,000 per financial year).
  2. 25% of Adjusted Total Income.
  3. Actual Rent Paid minus 10% of Adjusted Total Income.

2. Section 10(13A) vs Section 80GG Comparison

Feature Section 10(13A) (HRA) Section 80GG (Rent Deduction)
Target Assessee Salaried employees receiving HRA Self-employed & non-HRA salaried
Maximum Limit No statutory cap Max ₹5,000/mo (₹60,000/yr)
Mandatory Declaration Form 12BB (to employer) Form 10BA (on e-filing portal)

3. Electronic Filing of Form 10BA

Before filing your ITR-1, ITR-3, or ITR-4, you must log into eportal.incometax.gov.in, navigate to e-File > Income Tax Forms > File Form 10BA, enter the tenancy details, and e-verify using Aadhaar OTP.
