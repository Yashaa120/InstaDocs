About RentReceipt.online

Empowering tenants, salaried employees, and residential landlords worldwide with instant, professional, and 100% private in-browser document generation.

Our Origin & Core Mission

RentReceipt.online was founded with a singular conviction: generating routine financial and administrative documents—such as rent receipts, salary slips, and tenancy declarations—should never require handing over sensitive personal data to third-party marketing companies or commercial lead harvesters.

Across the web, most legacy document generators force users to register accounts, enter unverified mobile phone numbers, and disclose private landlord and banking information. That data is frequently logged to remote databases, aggregated into consumer marketing profiles, and sold to financial institutions or insurance brokers. RentReceipt.online fundamentally rejects this surveillance model. We engineered an open, accessible suite of high-speed document tools that run 100% locally within the user's web browser with zero server-side data retention.

The Zero-Server Storage Principle:

All PDF compilation, number-to-words currency transformations, signature rendering, and cryptographic QR code stamping occur in client memory using JavaScript (jsPDF and HTML5 Canvas). No personal names, PAN cards, SSNs, monthly rent amounts, or addresses ever leave your physical device.

Worldwide Tenancy & Tax Compliance Standards

While originally engineered to assist Indian taxpayers navigating Section 10(13A) House Rent Allowance (HRA) claims and Form 12BB declarations, RentReceipt.online has expanded into a global documentation standard supporting multiple currencies and jurisdiction-specific tenancy laws:

  • United States ($ USD): Formatted for American residential landlords, tenants, and real estate investors. Complies with state-by-state rent receipt laws (such as New York Real Property Law § 235-e and California Civil Code § 1499) and substantiates home office deductions on IRS Schedule C / Form 8829.
  • United Kingdom (£ GBP): Formatted in British Pounds with English words conversion. Adheres to the Tenant Fees Act 2019 and Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, providing compliant proof for Assured Shorthold Tenancies (AST), HMOs, and Universal Credit housing element claims.
  • Canada ($ CAD): Formatted for Canadian provincial tenancies (Ontario Residential Tenancies Act Section 109, BC RTA Section 26) and Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) reviews for the Ontario Trillium Benefit (OEHTC) and self-employed workspace deductions (Form T2125).
  • India (₹ INR): Complete compliance with the Income Tax Act, 1961, Section 10(13A), Rule 2A calculations, mandatory Landlord PAN validation under CBDT Circular No. 08/2013, and ₹1 revenue stamp markers for cash rents exceeding ₹5,000 under the Indian Stamp Act.
