About RentReceipt.online

RentReceipt.online is a free administrative and financial document generation suite built specifically for salaried employees, tenants, landlords, and small businesses in India.

Our Core Philosophy: 100% Privacy & Zero Data Storage

Unlike other platforms that require personal mobile numbers, emails, and account signups to harvest data, RentReceipt.online processes all document formatting directly in your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

None of your personal details, landlord PAN card numbers, salary breakdowns, or residential addresses are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers. When you close your browser tab, your session data is gone.

Who Builds & Maintains This Platform?

RentReceipt.online is developed and maintained by an independent team of Indian software engineers and financial technology professionals with deep domain background in enterprise payroll processing, tax calculation software, and statutory compliance under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Our document templates, tax deduction formulas, and receipt layouts are continuously audited against Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) circulars, Section 10(13A) compliance requirements, and Form 12BB employer submission standards used across major HRMS platforms in India (Darwinbox, GreytHR, Keka, ZingHR).

Our Tools

  • Rent Receipt Generator: Generate single-month or full 12-month PDF rent receipts for HRA tax exemption under Section 10(13A).
  • Salary Slip Generator: Create corporate-standard payslips with accurate Indian statutory deductions (PF, PT, TDS).
  • Affidavit Generator: Draft rental self-declarations and parent rent agreements.
