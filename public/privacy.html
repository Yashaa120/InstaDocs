Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Published by RentReceipt.online

At RentReceipt.online (accessible from https://www.rentreceipt.online), protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our worldwide visitors is our highest commitment. This Privacy Policy outlines the zero-data-retention technical framework governing our multi-currency rent receipt generator and salary slip tools, our cookie practices, our partnership with Google AdSense, and your statutory data rights under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and PIPEDA.

Core Privacy Summary:
  • Zero Form Data Transmitted: All document text, tenant names, landlord information, property addresses, and PAN numbers are processed solely in your browser memory.
  • No User Accounts Required: We do not ask for registration, login, phone numbers, or email credentials.
  • No Database Storage: No databases or cloud servers are connected to record financial data. When you close your browser tab, all session memory is permanently wiped.

1. 100% Client-Side In-Browser Execution

Unlike conventional web applications that upload your confidential tenancy and salary information to cloud servers, RentReceipt.online runs 100% locally on your computer or mobile device. All PDF compiling, rupee/dollar number-to-words conversions, and digital signature rendering take place inside your browser’s JavaScript engine (using jsPDF and HTML5 Canvas). No document parameters are ever sent across the wire to our servers.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Web Beacons

RentReceipt.online does not use first-party tracking cookies to build behavioral profiles. We may utilize browser localStorage exclusively to save non-sensitive UI settings (such as your chosen active currency e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, or INR, or your interface language). This preference data resides solely on your physical device and can be erased at any time through your browser cache settings.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policy

To keep our utilities, tax calculators, and statutory compliance guides 100% free for taxpayers and property managers worldwide without charging subscription fees, we partner with reputable third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

  • Google DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and other destinations across the web.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may freely opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party interest-based advertising via www.aboutads.info.
  • AdSense Policy Adherence: In strict compliance with Google AdSense Program Policies, advertisements are never served on screens without content, within behavioral popups, or disguised as tool actions. All advertising placements are clearly labeled "ADVERTISEMENT" with sufficient margins to prevent accidental clicks.

4. Server Log Files & Technical Diagnostics

Like standard internet hosting environments, our web servers automatically log basic network connection requests. These logs record Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These data points are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely for server security, DDoS defense, and infrastructure capacity planning.

5. European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you hold explicit rights under the GDPR and UK Data Protection Act:

  • Right of Access: You may request copies of your personal data. Because we store zero personal records on servers, we hold no user files to provide.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit any details directly inside the in-browser form inputs in real time.
  • Right to Erasure: Refreshing or closing your browser window instantly purges all inputs from device memory.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA)

Under California privacy legislation, California residents are entitled to know that RentReceipt.online does not sell, rent, or trade user personal information. We do not monetize personal data in any form.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

RentReceipt.online does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our tools are designed for adult tenants, salaried professionals, and landlords.
