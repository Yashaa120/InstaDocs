Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Zero Server-Side Data Storage

At RentReceipt.online, your personal and financial privacy is our highest priority. All document compilation, signature rendering, and PDF downloads are processed entirely client-side using JavaScript in your browser. We do not transmit, log, or store your tenant details, landlord names, PAN cards, rental amounts, or salary records on any database.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We may utilize standard analytics and advertising tools such as Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements that keep our service free. These third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on user visits.

3. Security

Because no data leaves your browser, your information is immune to remote server breaches or data leaks.

4. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries, email support@rentreceipt.online or view our Contact Us page.
