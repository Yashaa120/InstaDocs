Mandatory Details on a Valid Rent Receipt

To avoid tax rejection by your company's payroll department, your rent receipt must include:

  • Tenant Name: Must match your full legal name as per company records and PAN card.
  • Landlord Name: The property owner's full legal name.
  • Full Property Address: Clear house/flat number, building name, street, locality, city, and PIN code.
  • Rent Amount: Displayed clearly in both figures (e.g., ₹22,000) and words.
  • Rental Period: Specific start and end dates (e.g., 1st April 2025 to 30th April 2025).
  • Payment Mode: Clarify if paid via NEFT, UPI, Cheque, or Cash.
  • Landlord's PAN: Mandatory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 (₹8,333/month).
  • Revenue Stamp: A ₹1 revenue stamp affixed for cash payments exceeding ₹5,000.
  • Landlord's Signature: Physical or verified digital signature across the receipt or revenue stamp.

How to Calculate Your HRA Exemption

The exempt HRA amount under Section 10(13A) is the lowest of the following three values:

  1. Actual HRA received from your employer.
  2. 50% of Basic Salary + DA (for metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% (for non-metro cities like Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Gurgaon).
  3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of Basic Salary + DA.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I generate rent receipts for the entire financial year at once?

Yes! With RentReceipt.online, you can select the entire financial year (April to March) and download all 12 monthly receipts in a single multi-page PDF document formatted for instant printing.

Q: Is a revenue stamp required if I pay rent online via UPI or NEFT?

Under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, a ₹1 revenue stamp is only legally mandatory for cash transactions exceeding ₹5,000. For digital transfers (UPI, NEFT, IMPS), bank statements or transaction reference IDs serve as payment proof, although many HR teams still accept stamped receipts without objection.

Q: Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?

Yes, paying rent to parents is completely legal provided: (1) your parents legally own the house, (2) you execute a rental agreement and issue monthly rent receipts, (3) payments are made through bank channels, and (4) your parents report this rental income in their annual Income Tax Return (ITR).

Q: What if my landlord refuses to provide their PAN card?

If annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 and the landlord has no PAN, you must obtain a signed declaration along with Form 60 from the landlord stating their details and lack of PAN. If the landlord has a PAN but refuses to disclose it, you cannot claim HRA for the amount exceeding ₹1,00,000.
