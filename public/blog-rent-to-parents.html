1. The Legal Foundation: Is Paying Rent to Parents Permitted?

Yes. Under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and Rule 2A of the Income Tax Rules, 1962, any employee who receives a House Rent Allowance and actually incurs expenditure on rent for residential premises not owned by them is entitled to tax exemption.

The statute does not prohibit family members from entering into genuine tenancy contracts, provided:

  • The parent is the sole or co-registered legal title owner of the property.
  • The employee does not hold an ownership share on the property deed.
  • Actual monetary consideration is transferred through electronic banking channels (UPI, IMPS, NEFT).

2. Judicial Precedent: Ahmedabad ITAT Bajrang Prasad Ruling

Bajrang Prasad Ramdharani vs ACIT (ITA No. 138/Ahd/2013): The Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) ruled that HRA claimed for rent paid to parents cannot be disallowed merely because the parties are related. When genuine tenancy, rental agreements, and tax declarations by parents are established, the deduction is fully allowable under law.

4. The 6-Step Audit Checklist

  1. Verify that the title deed / municipal property tax receipt is in the parent's name.
  2. Execute an 11-month registered or notarized rental agreement.
  3. Transfer rent every month via bank transfer with “Rent” in the description.
  4. Issue monthly stamped rent receipts with landlord PAN.
  5. Parent must report the rental income under Schedule HP in their ITR.
  6. Do not withdraw cash immediately and hand it back to the child.
