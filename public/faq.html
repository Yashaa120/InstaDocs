Q2: What is the difference between a rent receipt and a rent slip for HRA?

In India, salaried employees and corporate payroll departments use the terms "rent receipt" and "rent slip" interchangeably. Both act as statutory documentary proof under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act to claim House Rent Allowance (HRA) tax exemption during annual tax declarations on Form 12BB.

Q4: Kitne rent par Landlord ka PAN card number lagta hai?

Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) circular ke mutabiq, agar aapka annual rent ₹1,00,000 (ya ₹8,333 prati mahina) se zyada hai, toh employer ko submit kiye jaane wale rent receipts par Landlord ka PAN card number dena mandatory hai. Agar rent ₹1 Lakh se kam hai toh PAN zaroori nahi hota.

Q5: Cash rent payment me revenue stamp kab lagana zaroori hai?

Indian Stamp Act ke mutabiq, agar aap monthly rent cash me ₹5,000 se adhik pay karte hain, toh receipt par ₹1 ka revenue stamp lagana aur uspe landlord ka signature lena anivarya hai. Online payment (UPI, NEFT, IMPS) ya cheque se payment par revenue stamp zaroori nahi hota.

Q6: Can I claim HRA if I pay rent to my parents?

Yes! Salaried individuals can legally claim HRA tax exemption by paying rent to their parents. The house must be registered in the parents' name, rent should be transferred through banking channels, monthly rent receipts must be issued, and parents must report this rental income in their annual ITR.

Q7: Can I claim HRA under the New Tax Regime?

No. Under Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime), HRA exemption is not available. To claim HRA exemption under Section 10(13A), you must choose the Old Tax Regime during income tax declaration.

Q8: What if my landlord does not have a PAN card?

If the landlord does not have a PAN card and annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000, you must obtain a signed declaration along with Form 60 from the landlord and submit it to your employer.
