What is a Salary Slip (Payslip)?

A Salary Slip (also known as a Payslip) is an official document issued by an employer to an employee every month. It details the employee's gross earnings, itemized allowances, statutory deductions, and the final net take-home salary credited to their bank account.

Salary slips are crucial financial documents required for:

  • Home & Personal Loan Applications: Banks require the latest 3 to 6 months of salary slips to verify debt-to-income ratios.
  • Credit Card Approvals: Financial institutions use payslips as official proof of steady monthly income.
  • Job Transitions: Future employers evaluate past compensation packages based on previous payslips.
  • Income Tax Filing & HRA Claims: Form 16 and ITR filing require accurate reconciliation with monthly pay components.
  • Visa Applications: Foreign embassies require payslips to confirm stable employment and financial ties.

Standard Salary Components in India

Earnings Component Standard Percentage / Nature Taxability
Basic Salary Typically 40% to 50% of Total CTC 100% Taxable
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Typically 40% (non-metro) or 50% (metro) of Basic Exempt under Section 10(13A) against rent receipts
Dearness Allowance (DA) Cost of living allowance (common in PSU / Govt) 100% Taxable
Special Allowance Balancing residual component of CTC 100% Taxable
Conveyance / Travel Allowance Commute expense component Taxable under New Tax Regime

Mandatory Deductions on an Indian Payslip

Deduction Type Standard Statutory Rate Governing Authority
Employee Provident Fund (EPF) 12% of (Basic Salary + DA) up to statutory wage ceiling EPFO (Ministry of Labour & Employment)
Professional Tax (PT) ₹150 to ₹200/month (state specific, capped at ₹2,500/year) Respective State Governments (e.g. Maharashtra, Karnataka)
Employee State Insurance (ESIC) 0.75% of Gross Wages (for wages ≤ ₹21,000/month) ESIC Corporation
Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Calculated as per employee's Income Tax slab Income Tax Department of India

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is a salary slip generated online legally valid?

Yes. A computer-generated salary slip that contains accurate corporate identification, employee credentials, PAN/UAN numbers, and authorized signatory details is legally valid and recognized by financial institutions across India.

Q: Can small businesses and startups use this tool?

Q: What is the difference between Gross Salary and Net Salary?

Gross Salary is the total amount earned before any deductions (Basic + HRA + DA + Allowances). Net Salary (take-home salary) is the actual amount credited to your bank account after subtracting statutory deductions (PF, PT, ESI, TDS) from Gross Salary.
