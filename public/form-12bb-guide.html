1. Purpose of Form 12BB

Introduced under Rule 26C of the Income Tax Rules, 1962, Form 12BB is the mandatory annual declaration furnished by salaried employees to their employers under Section 192 of the Income Tax Act. It provides statutory evidence for:

  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Rent paid, landlord name, residential address, and landlord PAN.
  • Leave Travel Concession (LTA): Boarding passes and travel bills for domestic journeys.
  • Deduction of Interest on Housing Loans: Section 24(b) certificate from bank or financial institution.
  • Chapter VI-A Deductions: Proof of investments under 80C, 80D, 80CCD, and other eligible sections.

2. Landlord PAN Thresholds in Form 12BB

As per CBDT Circular No. 8/2013, if your annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 per year (or ₹8,333 per month), reporting the landlord's Permanent Account Number (PAN) is legally mandatory. If the landlord does not possess a PAN, a signed declaration in Form 60 along with identity proof must be submitted.

3. Documentary Checklist for HRA in Form 12BB

  1. Signed monthly rent receipts for each month occupied during the financial year.
  2. Bank payment statements showing electronic transfers (NEFT, UPI, or cheque).
  3. Copy of the registered rental agreement or tenancy self-declaration affidavit.
  4. Landlord's PAN card copy or signed Form 60 declaration.
